What do you remember
holding in your hands?

Before streaming made everything infinite, holding an album meant something. Here's what we think got lost - and what VintageTape brings back.

The Object

The cassette was never just plastic and tape. It was a small piece of your life that you could hold, flip, and carry in your pocket.

The Time

Every rewinding sound was a reminder. Time didn't just pass - it left marks on the tape you could hear.

The Memory

Memories didn't hide in the cloud. They sat on shelves, in boxes, in carefully written tracklists you could touch.

The Ritual

Pressing play was never just a click. It was a small ceremony - the click of the button, the soft hum, the wait before the music began.

"Everything new is well-forgotten old."

The idea behind every VintageTape catalog.

Who this is for

Not for people chasing the newest release. For people who want one good playlist and a case they can put back on the shelf.
