FAQ

Before you write

Do I need an account?

No. VintageTape has no accounts or logins, and the form on this page is a demonstration only.

Which genres does VintageTape cover?

The Playlists page shows six genres: Rock, Noise Pop, k-pop, Neo, J-pop and Indie.

Is VintageTape a streaming app?

No. It is a themed catalog concept. It does not play music; it brings back the feel of flipping through a disc collection.

Is the form real?

No. It only shows how built-in HTML validation works. When all fields are valid, your browser opens a demonstration page. Nothing is sent or stored.
