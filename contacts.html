FAQ

Before you write

Do I need an account?

No. VintageTape has no logins. You can look at the demo registration form on the Register page, but it does not create an account.

Which genres does VintageTape cover?

The Playlists page shows six genres: Rock, Noise Pop, k-pop, Neo, J-pop and Indie.

Is VintageTape a streaming app?

No. It is a themed catalog concept. It does not play music; it brings back the feel of flipping through a disc collection.

What does the contact form check?

Name: at least 2 characters. Email: a valid address like name@example.com. Message: 10 to 500 characters. When everything is valid you see a demo message. Nothing is sent or stored.
